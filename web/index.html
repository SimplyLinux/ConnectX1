1
Download and extract the ZIP file from the link above.
2
Move ConnectX1.app to your /Applications folder.
3
Open Terminal and run:

This removes the quarantine flag since we don't yet have an Apple Developer certificate.
4
Launch ConnectX1 from Applications or Spotlight.
